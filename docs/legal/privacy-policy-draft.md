# Privacy Policy — approval draft

This document is not approved for publication. A qualified Brazilian legal
reviewer must replace the controller identity, address, DPO/contact details,
legal bases, international-transfer mechanism, processors, and effective date.

English Sprint AI processes Firebase account identifiers, consent records,
onboarding choices, mission state, structured learning evaluations, skill
evidence, subscription entitlement, minute usage, operational telemetry, and
support requests to provide and secure the service.

The product does not persist audio, PCM, SDP, provider call identifiers,
complete prompts, model reasoning, IP addresses, or raw installation
identifiers. Text retention is off by default. When explicitly enabled,
approved learning text is encrypted, key-versioned, and expires after 30 days.
Transient content required during a session expires within 60 minutes.
Content-free evidence, scores, state, security records, and billing records are
retained only for their approved operational or legal period.

Users can request an export and account deletion in the app. Deletion removes
the Firebase identity and scrubs retained learning and support content while
preserving only records that must legally remain in pseudonymous form. The
approved policy must state response times, complaint channels, ANPD rights,
processors including Google/Firebase/GCP, OpenAI, and RevenueCat, and where
processing occurs.
